Everything it can do

Every tool and trick, topic by topic. Each topic has a step-by-step tutorial with pictures.

Shapes that snap

Hold still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a clean line, circle, triangle or rectangle. Keep holding to turn and resize it.

Drag a colour to fill

Pull a colour out of the dock and drop it inside any closed shape. Drop it on a line or a text to recolour it.

Scratch out to delete

Scribble back and forth over a mistake, like on paper, and it disappears. Undo is right there if you change your mind.

Highlighter and laser

The highlighter slides under your writing so it stays crisp. The laser pointer leaves a glowing trail for presenting.

PDFs and sheets

Open a PDF or start a page of a set paper size, and write straight on top of it. Great for lecture slides and homework.

Text and stylus

Type wherever you need it, in five fonts and any size. Pressure sensitivity works with Apple Pencil and most Windows pens.

AI for handwriting

Select what you wrote: tidy it into typed text with proper maths, solve an equation with every step shown, or turn a function into a graph you can explore.

Your key never leaves your device

The AI uses your own API key. It stays in your browser and goes only straight to the provider you choose. There is no Whiteboard server, and boards, files and links never carry it.

Draw together, live

Share a link and whoever opens it draws on your board with you, in real time, each with a laser pointer in their own colour and name. Phones join by scanning a QR code. It goes straight between your browsers: no server, no account.

Getting started

  • Pick a tool and a colourThe dock at the bottom holds the pen, highlighter, eraser, text and more, with your colours beside them.
  • Set the brushThe sliders on the left set the thickness and the opacity. The button under them picks the line type.
  • Move aroundDrag with two fingers or scroll to move, pinch or Ctrl + scroll to zoom. “Fit to content” brings everything back into view.

Pen and colours

  • Pick a line typeThe button under the opacity slider picks solid, dotted, dashed, long dashes or dash and dot.
  • Mix any colourThe rainbow swatch opens the colour mixer, with HEX, RGB and HSL, and a pipette that picks a colour from the board.
  • Draw with pressureTurn on “Pressure sensitivity” in Settings: press harder with a stylus and the line gets thicker.

Highlighter, eraser, laser

  • HighlightThe highlighter has its own neon colours. It slides under the writing, so the writing stays crisp.
  • EraseThe eraser removes ink wherever it passes, and the slider on the left sets its size. A stylus’s eraser end works too.
  • Point with the laserThe laser pointer shows a glowing dot with a short trail, for presenting. It never draws, and dragging moves the board.

Shapes and colour

  • Hold still to snapEnd a stroke and keep still for a moment: it turns into a clean circle, line, triangle or rectangle.
  • Drag a colour inPull a colour out of the dock and drop it inside a closed shape to fill it.
  • Recolour a lineDrop a colour on a line, a text or a fill to change its colour. A dashed line stays dashed.

Selecting

  • SelectWith the select tool, tap something, or draw a loop around several things at once.
  • Move, resize, turnDrag inside the frame to move it, a corner or a side to resize it, the knob above it to turn it. Another finger, or Shift, snaps to 45°.
  • Change it laterWith the select tool, tap a shape. The bar under it changes its colour, thickness and line type.
  • Duplicate, keep, deleteThe bar at the top copies the selection, saves it as a sticker, or deletes it.

Scratch out and undo

  • Scratch it outScribble quickly back and forth over a mistake, like on paper, and it disappears. “Undo” appears at the bottom.
  • Undo and redoThe arrows at the top right undo and redo. On a touch screen, tap with two fingers to undo and with three to redo.

Text

  • TypeChoose the text tool and tap where the text goes. The bar above it sets the font, size and colour. Double-tap a text to edit it.
  • Handwriting into textSelect what you wrote, press “AI” and choose “Tidy up handwriting”: it becomes typed text, with the maths typeset properly.

AI: tidy, solve, plot

  • Add your own keyIn Settings → AI, choose a provider and paste your API key (“Get a key” shows where to make one; Gemini has a free tier). The key is kept only on your device and goes only to that provider.
  • Tidy up handwritingSelect your writing, press “AI” and choose “Tidy up handwriting”. It becomes typed text, with fractions, powers and roots typeset properly.
  • Solve with the steps“Solve, showing the steps” writes the working under the problem. The app then checks the answer itself by putting it back into the equation: ✓ means it fits.
  • Plot and explore“Plot as a graph” turns a written function into a graph. Double-tap it or press “Explore” to drag and zoom inside it; “Functions” changes what is drawn.

Pictures and stickers

  • Bring in a picture“Import…” places a picture, or the pages of a PDF, on the board.
  • Size and turn itA picture arrives selected: move it, resize it from its corners or sides, and turn it with the knob.
  • Stickers“Save as sticker” keeps a drawing to reuse. The sticker button in the dock places it on any board.

PDFs and sheets

  • Start a sheetIn the menu, “New sheet…” starts a page of a set paper size. To write on a PDF, press “Choose PDF…” there: its pages become the sheet.
  • Pick a paper sizeA and B sizes, North American paper, slides and squares, upright or sideways.
  • Write, highlight, add pagesWrite and highlight straight on the page. The + above or below the pages adds another one.
  • Add or remove pagesThe + above or below the pages adds one of the same size. While an end page is empty, a bin next to it deletes it.

Gallery and files

  • Keep boards in the gallery“Save to gallery” keeps your boards in folders, with previews. “Back up…” saves the whole gallery as one file.
  • Save to a file“Save as…” writes a .whtbd file wherever you like, and “Open from…” opens it again. Ctrl + S saves to the same place.
  • Back up and restore“Back up…” saves the whole gallery as one file, “Restore…” brings it back, and “Add boards…” brings in single board files.

Export and share

  • ExportPNG, JPEG, SVG, PDF and more. Every export carries the board inside it, so it can be opened and edited again.
  • Include the patternTurn on “Canvas pattern” to export the dots, lines or grid along with the drawing.
  • Share a link“Share link…” puts the whole board into a link and makes it live: whoever opens it gets a copy, and while your board is open, they draw on it with you. Nothing goes through a server.
  • Open a shared linkIn the installed app, which has no address bar, “Open share link…” opens a link someone sent you.

Live boards: draw together

  • Share and go live“Share link…” makes one link that carries a copy of the board and makes it live. A phone can join by scanning the QR code; on a computer, type the short code into “Open share link…”. “Stop” ends it; the board stays with you.
  • See each otherLines appear as they are drawn, and with the laser pointer everyone’s pointer shows, with their name: red for whoever started, a colour of their own for everyone who joined. Pointing at a graph you’re exploring puts your pointer on that spot of the curve for everyone.
  • See who’s whoTap the Live badge to see everyone on the board with their colour. Tap your own name to type a nickname; the others see it in the list and next to your pointer.
  • If it won’t connectSome school, university and mobile networks block direct connections. Then the link still opens a copy; or, if you have a TURN server of your own, enter it in Settings → Live boards.

Settings

  • Light, dark and canvas colourSettings sets the theme and the board’s colour: light, dark, following the theme, or any colour you mix.
  • Dots, lines or a gridThe canvas can be plain, dotted, lined like ruled paper, or a grid.
  • LanguageThe app speaks 14 languages and follows your device; you can pick another one here.
