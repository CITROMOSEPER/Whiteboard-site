    Feels like paper. Tidies up like software.

    The best ideas from drawing apps, in a calm glass interface that gets out of your way.

    Shapes that snap

    Hold still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a clean line, circle, triangle or rectangle. Keep holding to turn and resize it.

    Drag a colour to fill

    Pull a colour out of the dock and drop it inside any closed shape. Drop it on a line or a text to recolour it.

    Scratch out to delete

    Scribble back and forth over a mistake, like on paper, and it disappears. Undo is right there if you change your mind.

    Highlighter and laser

    The highlighter slides under your writing so it stays crisp. The laser pointer leaves a glowing trail for presenting.

    PDFs and sheets

    Open a PDF or start a page of a set paper size, and write straight on top of it. Great for lecture slides and homework.

    Text and handwriting

    Type where you need it, or write by hand and let it turn into text. Pressure sensitivity works with Apple Pencil and most Windows pens.

    Learn it in a few minutes

    Each step shows where to tap. Try it yourself as you go.
